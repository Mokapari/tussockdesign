The Handbook

The NZ Resilient Design Handbook

A reference book for architects, city planners, designers and licensed building practitioners. Something you look things up in, not something you read once and shelve.

Every chapter, the same shape

Every chapter follows one pattern, so you can find your way quickly:

  • The hazard or question
  • What the evidence says
  • The design responses, for rural new builds, urban new builds and urban renovations where they differ
  • What the rules require
  • Who does what: architect, planner, designer, builder
  • Policy that would help
  • A checklist, and the sources

Every response gets the same four questions. Does the house survive the event? Does it keep working through it? Can it recover and adapt after? Does it make the home more self-sufficient?
