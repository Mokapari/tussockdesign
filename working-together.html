Working together

What you'd get, and how it could work

You'd be my client and my mentor. The work answers to you, and you keep it grounded in what could actually get built.

What you get

A starting list. I'd shape it around what's most useful to you.

  • Design moves, drawn. The responses to each hazard as diagrams, ready to build on.
  • A hazard-and-response matrix. Which design moves suit which site and climate zone, at a glance.
  • A precedent library. Resilient houses from New Zealand and elsewhere, redrawn to one scale so they can be compared.
  • Consenting know-how. The consent pathways and rules each move runs into, in one place.
  • A monthly view of the work. A pin-up each month, like an architecture school crit, and a chance to steer it.
  • The Handbook. A reference book for the profession, made with you as client and mentor.
  • A brief for a design year. If you want to go further: a modular base house, designed against each hazard as a rural new build, an urban new build and an urban renovation.

The shape of it

  • About twelve months
  • About 30 hours a week
  • April 2027 to March 2028
  • A monthly pin-up, and a review at the end of each semester

What I'm asking of you

I've kept the ask small on purpose: an afternoon a month, for the pin-up. I know your time and energy matter, and I'll respect that. If you ever want to offer more, that's great, but it's not expected.

For you, it's a small ask. For me, it's an incredible opportunity.

The details, including who owns the work and how it's shared, are things we'd agree together. I'd hope to carry what I learn into my own design work later, and I'm glad to talk through what suits you.

Why this, and why now

I've wanted to do this research for a long time, and New Zealand is where I want to build my life and my practice. I'll be straight about the timing. A year of skilled work in New Zealand is also the last step in my residency application, and that has a firm deadline in October 2028. That's why the year needs to finish by mid-2028, and why I'm reaching out now.

About me

I'm an architectural designer. I have a Master of Architecture and eight years of practice in Canada.

Can I take a year of research through to a finished book? Yes. I've done it before. My thesis, Aging by Design (2016), looked at how seniors can age in place, in their own community. Since then I've written and published a series of books on the financial markets, and built a course and an app to go with them.

Next step

If any of this interests you, a conversation is the easiest next step, whenever suits. And if the timing isn't right, that's completely fine.
